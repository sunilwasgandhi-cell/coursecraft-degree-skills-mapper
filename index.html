ALUMNI IN THE STUDIO · SOUND ENGINEERING

From studio practice
to music production.

An alumni workshop connected classroom learning with the workflows and listening practices of contemporary music production.

ALUMNI WORKSHOP23 OCTOBER 2026

Learning by listening, making and mixing

CMR University welcomed Sound Engineering alumnus Jibril Jojo for a practical session with the existing Sound Engineering batches.

RESOURCE PERSONJibril Jojo, alumnus
TIME9:30 AM – 12:00 PM
FORExisting Sound Engineering batches
WRITTEN & CONDUCTED BYSunil Gandhi & Roger Walker

The workshop aimed to bridge academic learning with professional studio practice. Students explored modern music production workflows and strengthened their understanding of essential sound engineering techniques.

KEY AREAS OF FOCUS
  • Recording workflows
  • Mixing techniques
  • Monitoring and critical listening
  • Modern music production
  • Industry practices
✳

Learning outcomes
Students connected theory with practical audio production, developed critical listening skills, and gained insight into professional studio expectations.

FIELD NOTES / 23.10.26

Learning together, across generations.

Bringing an alumnus back into the studio gave students a chance to ask practical questions, hear how workflows translate to professional settings, and keep building their craft.

The session reflects CMR University’s commitment to experiential learning and alumni engagement, encouraging students to explore current developments and continue learning in music production.
